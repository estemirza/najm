Najmنجم

Islamic star patterns, constructed the way E. H. Hankin described in 1925. Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom.

How it works

Every pattern starts as a plain tiling of regular polygons. Turn on "Show tiling" to see it. From the midpoint of each edge, two lines leave at the contact angle and run inward until they meet the lines from the neighboring edges. Erase the tiling and the stars are what remain.

Hankin worked this out from patterns he studied in India and the Middle East and published it in 1925. Craig Kaplan turned it into an algorithm in 2005 ("Islamic star patterns from polygons in contact").

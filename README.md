# Najm

Islamic star patterns in the browser, built with E. H. Hankin's polygons-in-contact method (1925).

Live: https://estemirza.github.io/najm/

Pick a tiling of regular polygons, then set the contact angle: from the midpoint of every edge, two lines leave at that angle and run until they meet their neighbors. Erase the tiling and the stars remain.

- 7 tilings: 4.8.8, 6.6.6, 4.4.4.4, 3.3.3.3.3.3, 3.6.3.6, 3.12.12, 3.4.6.4
- Contact angle, gap, band width, scale, 5 palettes, show tiling, breathe
- Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom
- Save as SVG (Figma, Canva, Illustrator) or PNG
- Keys: Space breathe, T tiling, R surprise me

One HTML file, no dependencies.

References: Hankin, E. H. (1925). *The Drawing of Geometric Patterns in Saracenic Art*. Memoirs of the Archaeological Survey of India. Kaplan, C. S. (2005). Islamic star patterns from polygons in contact. *Graphics Interface 2005*.
